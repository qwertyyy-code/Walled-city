Pass-and-play tile laying. Offline after the first load. Base-game scoring included.

Computer opponents

Scoring

During the game

Road: when both ends are closed, 1 point per tile.

City: when its walls are closed, 2 points per tile and 2 per pennant. A city of only tiles scores points.

Monastery: when all 8 squares around it are filled, 9 points.

Whoever has the most meeples on a road or city scores it. Tied players all score in full. The meeples then go back to their owners.

End of the game

Unfinished road: 1 point per tile.

Unfinished city: 1 point per tile and 1 per pennant.

Unfinished monastery: 1 point, plus 1 for each tile around it.

Farmers: for each completed city, the player with the most farmers in the fields touching it scores points. Tied players each score .

Farmers stay on the board until the end of the game.

River: the river never scores. Meeples can't stand on the river, but can go on everything else on a river tile.
